What is Park(ing) Day?

Park(ing) Day is an annual event for people to reclaim urban space from cars to support art, activism, and cultural expression. It is an international event, and Beverly's entry will re-program two parking spaces on Cabot Street near Federal Street for art and play, with help from Montserrat College of Art, Beverly Main Streets, and other partners.
